# Community Code of Conduct

> Proposed community standard for n8n-Secureflow. The conduct contact and final operator
> identity still require owner confirmation.

We want this project to be a welcoming, evidence-driven place to build a local workflow scanner,
static catalogue, and reproducible release tooling. Participation is expected to be respectful,
curious, and practical.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate of different backgrounds, experience levels, and communication styles.
- Discuss code, data, and design decisions with evidence and without personal attacks.
- Give credit for contributions and preserve upstream attribution where it is required.
- Protect privacy: do not post credentials, private workflow JSON, personal data, or security
  reproduction details in public issues or pull requests.
- Accept a correction in good faith and help keep the project reproducible and accessible.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate
privacy violations, malicious disclosure of security information, impersonation, and sustained
disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Attempts to bypass provenance, rights, or release controls
are also out of scope for constructive contribution.

## Reporting and response

For conduct concerns, use the private conduct contact that will be published here after the owner
confirms it: `[conduct contact pending owner confirmation]`. Do not include sensitive details in a
public issue. If a report concerns security, privacy, copyright, or a takedown request, follow the
private-reporting instructions in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) or [`docs/takedown.md`](docs/takedown.md).

The future maintainer group will acknowledge reports, protect the confidentiality of the people
involved where reasonably possible, investigate proportionately, and communicate an outcome to
the reporter when appropriate. The project does not promise a response time until an owner and
response process are recorded.

This document is a community standard, not a legal opinion, a warranty, or a claim that a named
operator or independent reviewer has approved the repository.
